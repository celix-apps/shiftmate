Alarms & notifications

My alarm didn't ring. What should I check?
  1. Notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings › ShiftMate › Notifications.
  2. On iOS 26 or later, turn on ShiftMate › Settings › Notifications › System Alarms. With System Alarms on, alarms ring even when the app is closed, the phone is on silent or a Focus mode is active.
  3. Without System Alarms, alarms arrive as notifications: Silent mode, Do Not Disturb and Focus modes can mute them.
  4. Check the alarm is on and the date and time are correct in the Alarm tab or in the shift's alarm.
  1. ShiftMate needs three permissions: notifications, exact alarms and full-screen notifications. If one is off, a warning appears at the top of the Alarms tab; tap its button to turn it on.
  2. Settings › Privacy & GDPR › Manage Permissions opens the app's system settings.
  3. On some phones you also need to turn off battery optimization for ShiftMate.
  4. The alarm sound uses the alarm volume and plays in Do Not Disturb. Alarms are rescheduled automatically after a restart.
How do I set an alarm for a shift?

While adding or editing a shift, tap Add AlarmAlarms › Add Alarm. An alarm set before the shift moves with it. For alarms not tied to a shift, use the AlarmAlarms tab and the + button.

The free version includes one alarm; more alarms per shift are part of ShiftMate Pro.

Backup & sync

I'm switching phones. How do I move my shifts?

The easiest way is Google Drive: on the new phone, open Settings › Backup & Restore, connect the same Google account, pick a backup under View Backups and tap Restore. No Drive? Use a backup file:

  1. On the old phone: Settings › Backup & Restore › Create Backup File, then save the .shiftmatebackup file somewhere the new phone can reach (Files, iCloud Drive), or send it to yourself.
  2. On the new phone: Settings › Backup & Restore › Restore from Backup File and pick that file.
  3. Choose Merge to add it to what's already there, or Replace All to start from the backup (this can't be undone).

A backup includes shifts, leave, alarms, patterns, earnings settings and preferences. Apple HealthHealth Connect data is not included.

How does Google Drive backup work?

In Settings › Backup & Restore, tap Connect Google Drive. ShiftMate then backs up automatically once a day when you're online and keeps the last 5 backups. Backup Now backs up right away. The Automatic Backup switch on the main Settings screen turns daily backups on or off.

How does iCloud Sync work? Pro

Turn it on in Settings › iCloud Backup › iCloud Sync. Your shifts sync to every device signed in to the same Apple Account, through your own private iCloud storage. Changes take effect on the next app launch.

If you sign out of iCloud, the synced copy on that device is removed (it stays in iCloud). Make a backup file before signing out.

ShiftMate Pro

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Both the monthly and the yearly plan start with a 2-week free trial15-day free trial. The trial is for new subscribers and can be used once per Apple AccountGoogle account.

I paid but Pro isn't active.

Go to Settings › Subscription › Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple AccountGoogle account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by AppleGoogle Play. Open Settings › Subscription › Manage Subscription in ShiftMate, or iPhone Settings › [your name] › SubscriptionsGoogle Play › Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. If Pro ends, your settings are kept and come back when you subscribe again.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Refunds are handled by Google Play. Follow Google's steps at support.google.com/googleplay.

Widgets & calendar

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget (on iOS 17, the + button), search for ShiftMate and choose a size. For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen › Customize › Lock Screen › tap the widget area › ShiftMate.

Press and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets and find ShiftMate: Today's Shift, Weekly View, Weekly Overview, Weekly Earnings and Leave Balance. If a widget shows old information, open the app once; on some phones battery optimization can delay updates.

Can my shifts appear in my phone's calendar? Pro

Yes. In Settings › Calendar Sharing, turn on Add Shifts to Calendar, allow calendar access and choose a calendar (pick one from your Google account to see shifts on your other devices too). New and changed shifts appear there automatically. You can also share a month as a PDF or image.

Health & sleep

What does ShiftMate read from Apple HealthHealth Connect?

Only sleep data, and only if you allow it. It's used on your device to calculate the fatigue score and sleep plan, and never leaves your device.

You can change access any time in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › ShiftMate.

Connect it on Tracker › Health › Allow Health Access. Your watch or sleep app must write sleep data to Health Connect.

Privacy & your data

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud storage if you turn on iCloud Sync, and in your own Google Drive if you turn on Drive backup. We don't run servers that store your shifts. Details are in the privacy policyprivacy policy.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings › Privacy & GDPR › Delete My Account. This permanently deletes your shifts, alarms, settings and personal data and can't be undone, so make a backup first. An active subscription must be cancelled separately (see above); Google Drive backups are not deleted.
